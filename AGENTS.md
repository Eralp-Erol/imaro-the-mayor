- Source of truth: ULTIMATE-CODEX-PROMPT.md and `references/README.md`.
- Prime directive: Mayor influences autonomous private-sector urban development; never let player directly spawn private buildings.
- Visual: block/prism low-poly between Minecraft simplicity and Roblox vitality; distinguishable Aegean/Turkish everyday urban fabric. More simplified at runtime than reference images. True 3D, not image backgrounds.
- First playable: real parcels, zoning decision, market response/investor decision, temporal construction, budget/social reaction, pan/zoom/rotate, Turkish touch-first HUD.
- Small credible vertical slice > wide fake simulation. No fake HUD figures, dead buttons, or untested completion claims.
- Keep simulation time deterministic and independent from frame rate; ensure repeatable tests.
- Major deviation from art or core loop: fail fast, evaluate fresh start instead of patching wrong foundation.
- Capture real Godot screenshots and distinguish verified from unverified status.
- Don't commit secrets or push to remote without authorization.
